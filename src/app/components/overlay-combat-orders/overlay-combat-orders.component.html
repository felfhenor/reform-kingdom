Turns go in the order shown along the top. Pick a skill for the hero whose turn it is, then click its target.

Multi-target skills pick their extra targets at random, and targets marked (auto) are everyone the skill can reach.

Previously-used skills have a unique highlight color. Press enter to choose it again. If the previously-selected target doesn't exist, a new one will be chosen automatically.
